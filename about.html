About Me

George Plendl is a Senior Product Designer with 15+ years of experience turning complex B2B and eCommerce challenges into intuitive, high-impact digital experiences. Grounded in UX principles and guided by research, data, and user insights, I excel at transforming ambiguity into clarity, designing human-centered solutions that streamline workflows, spark innovation, and deliver measurable value for both users and businesses.

More recently, I've been integrating AI into my design process as a practical layer that improves clarity, consistency, and speed. I use it to synthesize stakeholder inputs and research, surface edge cases early, expand solution exploration, and accelerate everything from wireframing to spec writing. The result is sharper problem framing, faster iteration, and smoother handoff to engineering, without losing the human-centered foundation the work is built on.
